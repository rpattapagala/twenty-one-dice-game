Twenty One
How to play

Roll both dice to start. Then reroll one or both, or end your turn. The round leader gets up to three rolls and sets the limit for everyone else.

Read the higher die first. A 5 and a 3 score 53. Doubles score in hundreds, and 2–1 beats every other roll.
